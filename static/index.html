UNDERSTAND YOUR RECOVERY

Small deposits.
Real SOL.

Your token balance and your account’s SOL deposit are two different things.

Why is SOL locked?

Solana token accounts hold a SOL deposit to store their data. Closing an eligible account releases its remaining SOL.

What happens to tokens?

An empty account can be closed without burning tokens. A selected non-empty account needs its token balance permanently burned before closure.

How much do I receive?

The estimate deducts a 20% service fee from selected account deposits. Estimated network fees are included in the net estimate. Actual costs will appear in the checked recovery quote.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Questions before you recover?

Can I scan a wallet that is not mine?

Yes. Wallet account data is public. Only the wallet owner can authorize a recovery.

Does scanning cost anything?

No. Scanning is read-only and does not send a blockchain transaction.

Are all remaining tokens worthless?

No. A small balance can still have value. The initial scan estimates account deposits. The preparation pipeline then checks token prices and eligibility. You decide which balances you no longer want.

Can a burn be undone?

No. Burning permanently destroys the selected token balance. Do not select a token you want to keep.

Why are some accounts unavailable?

Supported stablecoin and SOL/WSOL balances are excluded from Dust. Empty stablecoin accounts can still be closed. The scan also applies initial account checks. Frozen accounts, different close authorities, native SOL balances, protected assets, NFT-like balances and unsupported extensions are excluded. Final mint and price checks belong to preparation.

Are the displayed rewards final?

No. This is an estimate based on selected account deposits minus the 20% service fee and estimated network costs. Actual costs and transaction eligibility still need to be verified.

Can I recover SOL from this page now?

Yes. Connect the scanned wallet, prepare your recovery and approve the checked transactions. Empty accounts are recovered first, followed by your selected Dust balances. Confirmed progress is saved so you can resume.
